Run a closed-loop sleep stimulation night, then analyse it

A hands-on course for sleep researchers and the people who run their nights. The first part follows a laboratory protocol for closed-loop auditory stimulation (CLAS) step by step: equipment, electrodes, the Portiloop Control Center and the night itself. The second part processes a real night recorded with that protocol, from the raw files to the brain's response to the sounds. Every concept comes with an interactive figure built from that night.

Thirty seconds of deep sleep from the tutorial night

The EEG at Cz during deep sleep, as the Portiloop recorded it. Each red line is a sound the device played, decided in real time from the signal: it detects a slow oscillation and plays a short burst of noise on its up-state (the positive phase). Tick Slow band to see the slow waves alone.

The tutorial night

The processing tutorials use one real night recorded by the lab with the protocol of these tutorials: 7.4 hours, 8 channels (ECG, EOG, chin EMG, Cz, Fz, Fpz) at 250 Hz, in the stimulation condition, with 1,930 sounds played by the "Sleep slow oscillations (upstate)" pipeline. The files were renamed and their metadata cleaned to remove the name of the person who was recorded. The interactive figures of the tutorials load summaries of this night from this site; to run the analyses yourself, download the night (about 200 MB) as described in Tutorial 05.

Hardware and software versionThe tutorials, photos and screenshots are for the Portiloop V2.3 (a Google Coral Dev Board Mini and the Portiloop board) with the software of the main branch, which recorded the tutorial night: replaying the night with it reproduces every one of its 1,930 stimulations at the same sample (Tutorial 08).
